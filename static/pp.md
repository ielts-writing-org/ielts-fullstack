## 2. Scope

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

The categories of data collected depend on the features you use and the information made available through your account.

### 3.1 Account and Authentication Data

When you sign in using GitHub OAuth or another supported authentication provider, we may process:

- Your account identifier within our Platform.
- Your identity-provider account identifier.
- Your username or display name, where provided.
- Your email address, where provided and requested by the authentication flow.
- Your profile image or other basic profile information, where obtained.
- Authentication and session information, including session identifiers and expiration details.

We use this information to authenticate you, maintain your session, associate your practice history with your account, and protect the Service.

We do not receive your GitHub password through a properly implemented OAuth flow.

The precise profile fields collected depend on the permissions requested and the authentication configuration.

### 3.2 Practice Sessions and Writing Content

When you use the writing-practice features, we may collect and store:

- IELTS task type and task prompt.
- Your essay or written response.
- Practice session identifiers and titles.
- Session creation and update timestamps.
- Practice settings and completion-related information.
- AI-generated evaluations and feedback associated with a practice session, where applicable.

Writing submissions may contain personal information if you include it yourself. Avoid including passwords, government identification numbers, financial information, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for writing practice.

### 3.3 Uploaded Images

For features that accept images for IELTS Writing Task 1 evaluation, we may process the images you submit.

Images may contain personal information or embedded metadata. Only submit images that you have the right to use and that are appropriate for the requested evaluation.

Images may be transmitted to infrastructure or AI-processing providers as necessary to perform the feature you request.

We do not claim that uploaded images are automatically stripped of metadata, deleted immediately, or excluded from all provider-side retention unless those protections have been verified.

### 3.4 Conversational AI Data

When you interact with the AI assistant, we may process:

- Your messages and prompts.
- Relevant writing responses or task context you provide.
- Relevant previous messages supplied to the model to maintain conversational context.
- AI-generated replies.
- Technical information required to manage the request and its response.

The amount of conversation history supplied to an AI model depends on the application's implementation.

### 3.5 Technical and Usage Data

When you access the Platform, we or our infrastructure providers may process technical information such as:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Request timestamps and URLs.
- Diagnostic information and error details.
- Security and abuse-prevention signals.
- Operational logs and performance metrics.

The information actually available to us depends on our hosting, logging, analytics, and security configurations.

We do not represent that the Platform uses analytics or advertising trackers unless such tools are actually deployed.

### 3.6 Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Platform may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain authentication sessions, preserve security, and support essential functionality.

Third-party authentication or infrastructure components may also use technologies necessary for their services.

We will provide any additional cookie notices, preference controls, or consent mechanisms required by applicable law, particularly if non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are introduced.
